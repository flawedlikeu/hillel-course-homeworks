Working Process

1. Discovery

We start with a short call to define goals, audience and scope so the whole team shares the same picture of the project before any design work begins.

2. Concept

I sketch several directions and moodboards, then narrow them down together with you into a single concept worth developing further.

3. Design

The chosen concept turns into detailed layouts, a component library and a clickable prototype ready for review and feedback.

4. Delivery

Final assets, source files and documentation are handed over, and I stay in touch to support the launch.
